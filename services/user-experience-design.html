OVERVIEW

Eliminating Cognitive Friction in Complex Enterprise Software

Enterprise software does not have to be painful or ugly. We bridge the gap between high-complexity technical workflows and intuitive, joyful digital ergonomics. Every interface is tested against real end-user task completion times and error rates.

By tokenizing your design variables—colors, typography scales, elevation depths, and border radiuses—we create a shared single source of truth between Figma and your frontend codebase that saves hundreds of developer hours.
