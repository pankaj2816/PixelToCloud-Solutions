Easy My Tax — High-Security CA & Tax Servicing Enterprise Portal

Automating tax calculation engines, client file workflows, and encrypted document storage for a premier Chartered Accountancy firm.

Client CA Pradeep Agarwal
Organization Easy My Tax
Industry Chartered Accountancy & FinTech
System Type Client Document Vault & Automated Tax Calculation Platform

The Challenge

Chartered Accountancy firms deal with massive volumes of sensitive financial records, strict tax deadlines, and frequent client inquiries regarding ITR, GST filings, and audit status. Easy My Tax previously relied on fragmented email exchanges, manual spreadsheet calculations, and physical document exchanges. This created operational bottlenecks during peak filing seasons, raised data privacy concerns, and slowed down client turnarounds.

The Engineering Solution

PixelToCloud engineered a custom, bank-grade web portal built specifically around the daily operating workflows of CA Pradeep Agarwal and his practice. We designed an automated tax and GST calculation engine, a secure client document locker with 256-bit AES encryption, and real-time filing progress tracking. Clients can securely upload bank statements, view computed tax liability previews, and download finalized acknowledgment forms without back-and-forth emails.
