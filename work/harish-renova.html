Harish Renova & Revital Physiocare — Clinical Healthcare Portals

Streamlining oncology patient consultation bookings, physiotherapy appointments, and clinical exercise guidance with instant WhatsApp notifications.

Client Dr. Neeraj Kumar Rathee & Dr. Archit Joshi
Organization Harish Renova Cancer Center & Revital Physiocare Lab
Industry Healthcare & Clinical Services
System Type Patient Scheduling, Telehealth & Clinical Guidance Systems

The Challenge

Specialized healthcare providers—such as oncology centers and physiotherapy rehabilitation clinics—require websites that instill immense trust, maintain high patient privacy, and load instantly even on low-bandwidth mobile devices. Patients experiencing distress or pain need frictionless appointment scheduling without complicated account creation or confusing navigation.

The Engineering Solution

PixelToCloud designed and deployed fast, accessible, and reassuring web platforms for both Harish Renova Cancer Center (Dr. Neeraj Kumar Rathee) and Revital Physiocare Lab (Dr. Archit Joshi). The platforms feature instant calendar slot selection, automated WhatsApp appointment confirmations, direct emergency routing, and clear patient guidance libraries for post-treatment exercises and recovery.
