Nebula3D — Hardware-Accelerated 2D/3D WebGL Configurator

Enabling 60 FPS hardware-accelerated 3D product visualization, interactive geometry manipulation, and PBR lighting inside the web browser.

Client Industrial & Spatial Design Initiative
Organization PixelToCloud Graphics Lab
Industry 3D Spatial Software & WebGL Engineering
System Type Interactive Browser 3D CAD & Model Visualizer

The Challenge

Traditional web-based product configurators and 3D visualizers often struggle with sluggish frame rates, heavy asset download sizes, and clunky mobile touch interaction. Most businesses are forced to choose between static 2D images or bloated third-party viewer plugins that create high bounce rates and poor mobile usability.

The Engineering Solution

PixelToCloud engineered Nebula3D from the ground up using custom WebGL shaders and Three.js. The engine provides buttery-smooth 60 FPS rendering across both desktop and mobile screens, zero-plugin browser compatibility, dynamic studio lighting, customizable PBR material shaders, wireframe toggles, and real-time CAD-like orbit, pan, and zoom controls.
