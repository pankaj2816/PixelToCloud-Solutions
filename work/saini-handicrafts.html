Saini Handicrafts — Bespoke Artisanal E-Commerce Boutique

Elevating handcrafted luxury with immersive storytelling, 360° product zoom inspectors, and multi-currency global checkout.

Client Jyoti Saini
Organization Saini Handicrafts
Industry Art, Craft & Luxury Retail
System Type High-Converting Digital Boutique & Multi-Currency Store

The Challenge

Selling bespoke handcrafted artwork and artisanal products online requires conveying subtle textures, material quality, and the story behind each creation. Standard e-commerce templates failed to communicate the craftsmanship of Saini Handicrafts, and rigid off-the-shelf cart plugins slowed down the user journey and added unnecessary transaction fees.

The Engineering Solution

PixelToCloud engineered a tailor-made digital storefront that pairs editorial-style visual storytelling with custom interactive product inspectors. Customers can zoom into fine craft details with a high-resolution canvas magnifier, switch dynamically between international currencies (USD, INR, EUR, GBP), and complete custom commission requests through an intuitive step-by-step order builder.
