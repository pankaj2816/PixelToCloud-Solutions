Easy My Tax — High-Security Client Portal & Tax Calculation System

The Challenge

Easy My Tax manages sensitive financial records, ITR filings, and GST audits for hundreds of business clients. Handling these workflows through unencrypted email attachments and manual spreadsheets created severe turnaround delays and data security vulnerabilities during filing deadlines.

What PixelToCloud Built

We engineered a dedicated client portal featuring an automated GST and tax calculation engine, encrypted client document storage, milestone return trackers, and automated client billing workflows.

Verified Outcome

Automated return calculations and structured file vaults transformed multi-day email chains into same-day review turnarounds for client returns.

How We Work

A structured, low-risk engagement process from initial discovery to long-term operations.

STEP 01

Discovery & Workflow Analysis

We audit your operational processes, data requirements, and pain points to define exact business objectives before writing any code.

STEP 02

Architecture & Fixed-Scope Plan

You receive a clear technical architecture, database schema overview, and a transparent milestone roadmap with fixed deliverables and timelines.

STEP 03

Sprint Build & Staging Reviews

We build your platform in structured iterations. You test working software in private staging environments at every milestone review.

STEP 04

Launch & Long-Term Support

We handle cloud deployment, server hardening, and repository handover. We back every project with ongoing engineering maintenance.
