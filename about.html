Privacy & Data Confidentiality

Client Privacy & Data Security Commitment

At PixelToCloud Solutions, we hold client confidentiality, proprietary software logic, and infrastructure data to bank-grade standards.

1. Zero Data Harvesting: We do not track, sell, or profile your business. Public API queries performed on our developer playground (DNS/IP probes) are processed strictly on client-side requests and never stored.
2. Non-Disclosure by Default: All conversations, source code blueprints, database schemas, and business metrics shared during project discovery are protected under mutual non-disclosure principles.
3. Direct Client Infrastructure: All production servers, domains, AWS accounts, and Docker registries are configured under your direct ownership. We never hold client assets hostage.
